Each subcategory contains 20 names, alphabetized within its section.

## Historical background references

The following sources informed the broad camp vocabulary and purposes, not the invented names themselves:

- [Caravanserai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caravanserai): shelter along trade routes; a permanent caravanserai and a temporary caravan camp are different kinds of site.
- [Castra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castra): Roman military camps, used here only as a historical precedent for organized encampments.
- [Gazetteer of Markets and Fairs to 1516](https://archives.history.ac.uk/gazetteer/intro.html): medieval trading and gathering contexts.
