# 500 City Names

**Category:** City  
**Entries:** 500  
**Researched:** 9 October 2026

500 real European city/town names for medieval worldbuilding, selected from online urban lists. Modern and well-known historical forms are retained. The source lists establish the names and urban classifications; they do not verify that every location was already a city in 500–1500. Medieval founding dates and city status remain unverified individually. Clearly modern planned settlements were excluded where identified.

Names are alphabetized and unique within this file after ignoring accents, punctuation and case. A historical place can appear in different category files; settlement roles and sizes changed over time. County labels identify the source context, not a claim about exact modern boundaries.
