# 500 Homestead Names

**Category:** Homestead  
**Entries:** 500  
**Researched:** 9 October 2026

500 historic Norwegian farm-name forms taken from the farm-name section of Oluf Rygh's Norske Gaardnavne, volume 19. This late nineteenth-/early twentieth-century compilation includes older and medieval forms, but each selected farm's medieval existence has NOT been individually established. Treat these as sourced traditional farmstead/homestead naming inspiration. Historical spellings, including Aa, are retained. The online transcription is OCR; obvious corruptions were excluded, but a diplomatic transcription of the scanned originals has not been performed.

Names are alphabetized and unique within this file after ignoring accents, punctuation and case. A historical place can appear in different category files; settlement roles and sizes changed over time. County labels identify the source context, not a claim about exact modern boundaries.
