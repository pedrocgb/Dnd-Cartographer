# 500 Town Names

**Category:** Town  
**Entries:** 500  
**Researched:** 9 October 2026

500 real place names matched to Domesday county indexes (1086) and to the Gazetteer of Markets and Fairs to 1516. This is a medieval market-settlement name bank: holding a market or fair did not automatically make a place a chartered town. Modern spellings are used; the category is intended for worldbuilding.

Names are alphabetized and unique within this file after ignoring accents, punctuation and case. A historical place can appear in different category files; settlement roles and sizes changed over time. County labels identify the source context, not a claim about exact modern boundaries.

Market/fair cross-check: Samantha Letters, [Online Gazetteer of Markets and Fairs in England and Wales to 1516 — Index of Places](https://archives.history.ac.uk/gazetteer/places.html). The individual name spellings below are independently grounded in the linked Domesday indexes.
