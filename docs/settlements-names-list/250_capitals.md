# 250 Metropolis / Capital Names

**Category:** Metropolis / Capital  
**Entries:** 250  
**Researched:** 9 October 2026

250 sourced historical capital, royal-seat, and major urban-centre names for medieval worldbuilding. Capitals of smaller kingdoms and principalities qualify; metropolis is not treated as a modern population threshold. Historical periods vary, and some royal seats predate the Middle Ages. Historical aliases may represent the same physical city in different periods; these are distinct name options, not 250 distinct archaeological sites.

Names are alphabetized and unique within this file after ignoring accents, punctuation and case. A historical place can appear in different category files; settlement roles and sizes changed over time. County labels identify the source context, not a claim about exact modern boundaries.
