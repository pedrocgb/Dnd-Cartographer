## How to use this collection

Choose a first name, optionally a second given name, and optionally a surname or clan name. **Middle names are optional** and are not asserted to be an established custom for every culture. Avoid repeating the same name within a generated full name. Gender labels describe this pool’s selection defaults, not restrictions on characters.

Each name is a separate table row. The **Name** column is the value to import; the remaining columns are metadata. Numbering restarts at 1 in every section. All four lists contain exactly 500 entries. Case, accents, spaces, and punctuation are normalized for duplicate detection across this file.

These are selections from real-world naming pools in **Faker 40.41.0**, not invented fantasy syllables. Locale labels identify the dataset’s usage region, **not a name’s exclusive origin or ethnicity**. The list spans North America, Latin America, Europe, and Asia. It is not restricted to names attested in 500–1000 CE.

Compound first names were omitted so optional second names can be combined cleanly. Human middle-name entries are additional given names selected from the same regional datasets, not proof that the source culture uses middle names. Some cultures put family names first, use multiple surnames, or do not require a hereditary surname. Preserve those choices in your app.

Japanese readings come from the provider’s paired romanized names. Chinese and Korean Latin display forms are **automatic, approximate transliterations**, without tone marks or a promised official romanization standard; their original spellings are retained. Preserve the original spelling if exact orthography matters. Latin-script diacritics are retained.

Source status: **2,000 dataset-sourced entries; 0 invented entries.** These are dataset-level attestations, not 2,000 separately researched biographical records.
